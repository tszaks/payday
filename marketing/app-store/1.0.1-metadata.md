## Description

Track cash and credit tips from every shift. See what this pay period is earning, which shifts usually pay best, and whether your paystub includes the tips and overtime you earned.

Built for servers, bartenders, baristas, delivery drivers, stylists, and anyone whose income changes shift to shift.

LOG A SHIFT IN SECONDS
Cash and credit together, one number for the shift. Start a shift from the app, Control Center, the Home Screen, or Siri and Payday times it for you, then fills in your exact hours when you close out.

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
Your pay period total, how you are running against your usual pace, and how long until payday. A Live Activity keeps the running clock on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while you work.

CHECK YOUR PAYCHECK
Payday predicts the tips line before your check arrives, then reads the stub with you: tips, regular wages, overtime, gross, taxes, and net. It names any gap it finds, including overtime your hours earned but the stub did not pay.

FIND THE SHIFTS THAT PAY
Payday shows the range half your shifts land in, so you can see what is reliable and what counts as a good night. Every read states how many shifts it is based on, and it stays quiet when the data does not support a claim.

PLAN THE WEEK
See a projection of next week built from the nights you actually work, with its own track record so you know how much to trust it.

YOUR NUMBERS, YOUR CONTROL
No ads. No in-app analytics or advertising trackers. Your earnings are never sold or shared. Sign in with Apple keeps your shifts backed up and on every device. Export everything to CSV or delete your account inside the app.

Log your next shift with Payday and know what your work actually paid.
