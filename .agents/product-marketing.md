# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-09-19*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Payday helps tipped workers log each shift, understand what they are earning, and check whether their paycheck includes the tips and overtime they earned.

**What it does:** Payday combines fast cash and credit tip logging with pay-period totals, shift-level insights, projections, paycheck verification, widgets, Live Activities, and private on-device earnings calculations. It turns the end of a shift, the days between shifts, and payday itself into one connected loop.

**Product category:** Tip tracker and earnings tracker for tipped workers.

**Product type:** Consumer iPhone utility.

**Business model:** Free in the current App Store listing, with no in-app purchases.

## Target Audience

**Primary users:** Servers, bartenders, baristas, delivery drivers, stylists, and other hourly workers who receive tips.

**Primary use case:** Record a shift in seconds and know what it contributed to the current pay period.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Tell me whether tonight was good.
- Tell me whether this pay period is on track.
- Help me check whether my employer paid the right tips and overtime.
- Show which shifts, weekdays, and patterns actually earn the most.

**Use cases:**

- Log cash, credit, tip-out, wages, hours, and optional receipt details after a shift.
- Compare a completed shift with the user's own history.
- Review pay-period totals and projected earnings.
- Enter a paystub and compare it with Payday's expected amount.
- Check earnings quickly through widgets, Siri, Shortcuts, and Live Activities.

## Problems and Pain Points

**Core problem:** Tipped income is fragmented across cash, card tips, tip-outs, hourly wages, overtime, and paychecks. Workers often know what happened tonight but cannot see what the whole pay period should add up to.

**Why alternatives fall short:** Notes and spreadsheets require manual math. Bank balances arrive too late and mix income with spending. Generic budget apps do not understand shifts, tip-outs, or the paycheck tips line.

**What it costs users:** Missed pay discrepancies, uncertainty between paydays, weak visibility into which shifts pay best, and repeated manual bookkeeping.

**Emotional tension:** “Was tonight actually good?” “Am I on track?” “Did they pay me correctly?”

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Tip-tracking and shift-income apps. Competitor-specific gaps require current market research before publication.

**Secondary:** Spreadsheets, Notes, POS screenshots, payroll portals, and personal-finance apps.

**Indirect:** Memory and waiting for the paycheck to arrive.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Connects each shift to a pay period and paycheck verification.
- Treats cash, credit tips, tip-out, wages, overtime, and mandatory gratuity as distinct facts.
- Uses the user's own history for records, pace, projections, and shift insights.
- Keeps core earnings math deterministic and private.
- Offers native Apple surfaces such as widgets, Siri, Shortcuts, Live Activities, and Face ID protection.

**How Payday works differently:** It is built around the worker's real cycle: close a shift, understand the period, verify the paycheck, and learn over time.

**Why that is better:** The user gets an answer immediately after logging and can later verify the employer's math from the same underlying record.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| I already use Notes or a spreadsheet. | Payday removes the repeated math and connects each shift to the pay period and paycheck. |
| I do not want financial details exposed. | Payday uses private storage and deterministic calculations for core earnings data, with optional Face ID protection. |
| Logging every shift sounds tedious. | The primary flow is designed to take under ten seconds, with optional details kept optional. |

**Anti-persona:** Salaried workers without variable shift income, businesses seeking payroll software, and users looking for budgeting or tax filing rather than earnings tracking.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Manual math, missing context, uncertainty about paychecks, and scattered records.

**Pull:** Fast shift logging, a clear pay-period number, personal patterns, and paystub verification.

**Habit:** Existing Notes or spreadsheet routines and the belief that memory is good enough.

**Anxiety:** Setup effort, data privacy, accuracy, and whether another daily-use app will become a chore.

**Words to use:** tips, shift, cash, credit, tip-out, pay period, paycheck, paystub, wages, overtime, earned, logged.

**Words to avoid:** optimize your finances, wealth, hustle, grind, gamified, effortless money.

**Glossary:**

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Shift | One completed work period and its earnings facts. |
| Pay period | The employer's date range that rolls into one paycheck. |
| Tips line | The tips amount shown on a paycheck or paystub. |
| Tip-out | Money shared with support staff and removed from take-home tips. |
| Reveal | The short result shown immediately after a shift is logged. |
