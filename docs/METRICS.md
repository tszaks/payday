### 1.1 Definitions per MetricID

Conventions used below. All money is integer cents. "Work date" means the civil day the user logged the shift on, interpreted in the payroll time zone frozen on the `PayrollCalendarPolicy` in effect for that day (Design 1), never the device zone. "Pay period" means the `PaySchedule` period whose inclusive day range contains the shift's work date. "Pay date" means the date printed on the paycheck stub (a `PaycheckRecord` matched to a period by its `periodEnd`). `asOf` is an inclusive civil-day cutoff applied to the end of any range; it clamps exactly as `StatsEngine.periodToDateTotal` does today (shifts on days after `asOf` are excluded) and applies to tips AND wages together. Every aggregate is a plain sum of per-shift `EarningsComponents` produced once by `CompensationLedger`; no consumer recomputes a component from stored rows.

**earnedIncome.** Formula: `voluntaryTips + gratuityFees - tipOut + regularWages + overtimeWages`, where each term is the sum of that component over the selected shifts and the wage terms come from the ledger's per-shift allocation. Date basis: work date; scopes are shift, day, calendar month, pay period, year to date, or an arbitrary `DayRange`, each optionally clamped by `asOf`. Missing-data rule: a shift whose wage valuation is `.unavailable(.rateNotSet | .hoursMissing | .noCalendarPolicy)` contributes its non-wage components and zero wages; the `EarningsResult` reports this through `completeness` and distinguishes `knownComponents` (all selected shifts) from `coveredComponents` (only shifts with hours). A shift with hours but no tips still counts (fixture Z1). Allowed labels: "Total" when `.complete`, "Known so far" when `.partial`, "You kept" when the selection's tipOut > 0, and "Earned" (reserved for this metric; see 1.3 on the current collision). Completeness presentation, from Design 2: `.complete` shows the plain amount labelled "Total"; `.estimated` shows the plain amount plus the caption "Wages estimated from your current rate" until the rate-history prompt is answered; `.partial(missingHours:missingRate:)` shows `knownComponents.earnedIncomeCents` with the caption "wages missing for N shifts" (or "no rate set" when that is the cause), replaces "Total" with "Known so far", renders partial days in charts as hollow bars, and makes $/hr show "N of M shifts"; `.off` (wage feature disabled or no rate policy) renders the non-wage amount labelled "Tips" or "Tips & gratuity" as defined under nonWageEarnings; `.noShifts` renders no currency figure.

**nonWageEarnings.** Formula: `voluntaryTips + gratuityFees - tipOut`, the same selection and `asOf` semantics as earnedIncome with the wage terms removed. Date basis: work date. Missing-data rule: none; this metric is always computable for any shift and carries `completeness` only so callers can tell whether a wage-inclusive figure was available. Allowed labels: "Tips" when the selection's gratuityFees == 0, otherwise "Tips & gratuity". It is the `.off` presentation of earnedIncome and the fallback basis for any analytics comparison that includes a wage-incomplete observation (Design 2: no comparison ever mixes complete and incomplete observations; when it falls back the copy says so).

**voluntaryTips.** Formula: `cash + credit` after `earningsSchemaVersion` normalization: under version 2 the stored amount is already voluntary-only; under legacy version 1 the single receipt-metrics owner row's amount is `max(0, amount - gratuityFeesCents)` and every other row is its stored amount. Reported as a whole and as its cash and credit sub-components. Date basis: work date. Missing-data rule: none. Allowed labels: as today's drawer rows, "Cash tips", "Credit tips", and "Tips" for the sum in lip text. Presentation: never shown as an "earnings" figure on its own.

**gratuityFees.** Formula: the employee-paid mandatory gratuity and fees from the shift's single canonical receipt payload (`employeeGratuityFeesCents`), counted once per shift regardless of how many legacy rows carry a copy of the payload (fixture N2). Date basis: work date. Missing-data rule: none; 0 when no receipt. Allowed labels: "Gratuity & fees" (drawer row), "Gratuity" (lip text and notification clause).

**tipOut.** Formula: the shift's tip-out as stored once on the shift (after migration, `ShiftRecord.tipOutCents`; before it, the credit row's value if present, else the cash row's, never summed across rows, fixture N1). Reported as read, never derived as gross minus net. Date basis: work date. Missing-data rule: `nil` (never entered) is distinct from 0 and exports as an empty cell; aggregates treat `nil` as 0. Allowed labels: "Tipped out" (rendered with a leading minus in drawers), "tipped out" (belief line).

**regularWages.** Formula: from `CompensationLedger` (Design 1): shifts are grouped into workweeks by `PayrollCalendarPolicy.workweekStartWeekday` over ALL shifts (never the caller's range), ordered within the week by work day, period rank, recordedAt, id; each shift's minutes are split chronologically at the 2400-minute threshold; regular cents are allocated per shift by cumulative half-up rounding of `rate x minutes` so that the per-shift values telescope to the exact weekly total (fixture W1: 1203 + 1556 = 2759, not 2760). The rate is the `PayRatePolicy` in effect on the work day. Date basis: work date; a shift is attributed whole to its work day and never split across a workweek boundary, so month and pay-period sums are additive (fixture W2). The calendar-grid `PaySchedule.firstWeekday` has no effect (fixture W3). Missing-data rule: `.unavailable(.rateNotSet)` when no rate policy covers the day (minutes still count toward the threshold), `.unavailable(.hoursMissing)` when the shift has no minutes, `.unavailable(.noCalendarPolicy)` when no calendar policy covers the day; each rolls into `completeness`. A rate policy with `provenance == .assumedFromLegacySetting` marks the valuation `assumed` and the result `.estimated`. Allowed labels: "Wages · Xh Ym" where the hours label is the regular minutes formatted as whole hours and minutes (fixture E1 governs hour formatting: minutes are literal, never rounded to quarter hours).

**overtimeWages.** Formula: as regularWages for the minutes past the 2400-minute threshold within the workweek, priced at `rate x minutes x 150 / 100` with the same cumulative rounding; the per-shift overtime share is carried on the shift that crossed the threshold (fixture W2: Thu 849, Fri 2547). Date basis, missing-data rule and completeness: identical to regularWages. Allowed labels: "Overtime · Xh Ym", shown only when the selection's overtime cents > 0.

**expectedPaycheckTipsLine.** Formula: over the shifts of one pay period, `base = credit > 0 ? credit : cash + credit` and `tipsLine = max(0, base - tipOut)` (today's `PredictedPaycheck.tipsLineCents`, kept as the contract). Date basis: pay period, all shifts by work date within the period, no `asOf` (an open period reports the expectation from shifts so far and the copy must say so). Missing-data rule: none (no wage input). Allowed labels: "Your check's tips line".

**expectedPaycheckGross.** Formula: `expectedPaycheckTipsLine + gratuityFees + regularWages + overtimeWages` over the same pay period. Date basis: pay period. Missing-data rule: wages `.unavailable` contribute zero and the result carries `completeness`; a `.partial` or `.off` expectation must not be presented as a firm figure without the completeness caption. Allowed labels: "Expected".

**observedPaidTips.** Formula: the stub's tips line exactly as stored on `PaycheckRecord.paidTipsCents`, no inference applied anywhere it is read, exported, or synced. Date basis: pay date (the record's own `periodStart`/`periodEnd`/pay date, matched to a pay period by `periodEnd`). Missing-data rule: absent when no paycheck record matches the period. Allowed labels: "Paid", "check paid".

**proposedPaidTipsCorrection.** Formula: `inferred = grossPay - regularWages - (overtimeWages ?? 0) - (gratuity ?? 0)`; a proposal exists only when `regularWages` and `grossPay` are present, `inferred >= 0`, `inferred != paidTips`, and `|inferred - paidTips| <= 100` cents. It is a separate value from observedPaidTips, never auto-applied, never written back to the record, and never substituted in a comparison unless the user accepts it (fixture P1: observed 10000, proposed 10050, observed unchanged). Date basis: pay date. Allowed labels: "Looks like $X (accept?)".

**reconciliationDelta.** Formula: `observed - expected`, computed per component and never summed across components: tips = observedPaidTips - expectedPaycheckTipsLine; gratuity = stub gratuity - gratuityFees; regular wages = stub regular wages - regularWages; overtime wages = stub overtime wages - overtimeWages; gross = stub gross - expectedPaycheckGross. Date basis: pay period on the expected side, pay date on the observed side. Missing-data rule: a component is absent when either side is absent; a wage component is absent when computed wages are `.unavailable`; the delta is never added into any income figure. Allowed labels: "checked"; the figure is red only when < 0. Tolerance is not part of the registry; today's code uses 5c in `PaycheckAudit`, 100c for wages, and 0c in the Periods list and Period detail, and unifying these is an open decision recorded in 1.3.

**hourlyRate.** Formula: `coveredComponents.earnedIncomeCents * 60 / minutes` with half-up rounding, where both numerator and denominator are taken over the covered shifts only (shifts with `minutesWorked`); a shift with tips and no hours is excluded from both sides (fixture H1: 2000c/h, not 4000). Date basis: inherits the selection's scope. Missing-data rule: `nil` when covered minutes == 0; always carries `N of M` coverage counts. Allowed labels: "Averaging $X/hr · N of M shifts". Analytics that compute a $/hr on a non-wage basis (StatsEngine tiles and Moves today) must declare `nonWageEarnings` as their basis in copy and are tagged `analytics:*` in the inventory, not `hourlyRate`.

### 1.2 Tags used in the inventory that are not MetricIDs

- `presentation-only`: the figure is either not money (counts, hours labels, progress fractions, elapsed clocks), a static sample or onboarding projection, a pass-through formatter, a stored stub observation with no registry row yet, or a subtotal that is a plain sum of registry components (for example gross before tip-out = voluntaryTips + gratuityFees + regularWages + overtimeWages). These rows carry no cents of their own and must be derived from `EarningsResult` fields, never computed from stored rows.
- `analytics:<name>`: a `StatsEngine` comparison, projection, or ratio (pace, typical range, trend, Moves, tiles, forecast accuracy, tip percent, receipt performance). Each must declare its basis (`earnedIncome` or `nonWageEarnings`) and, per Design 2, may use `earnedIncome` only when every observation in the comparison is wage-complete.

### 1.3 Registry gaps surfaced by the inventory (decisions owed before PR 5)

- Gross before tip-out (Dashboard and Period detail "Earned" lip and row) has no MetricID, and its label "Earned" is the label the registry assigns to earnedIncome. Either add a `grossEarnings` row or rename the subtotal.
- Observed stub fields other than paid tips (regular wages, overtime wages, gratuity, gross, taxes, net, hourly rate, owed tips) have no registry rows, yet reconciliationDelta needs them as its observed side per component.
- reconciliationDelta tolerance (0c, 5c, 100c in three places today) is unspecified.
- Tips plus gratuity before tip-out (`gross_tip_earnings_cents` on the API) equals nonWageEarnings + tipOut and has no dedicated row.

## 4. Contract

The governing rule, from the plan:

> The same metric, date scope, cutoff, source revision, compensation policy, and engine version must return the same integer-cents result and the same completeness state on every consumer.

What that binds, concretely:

1. **One valuation per shift.** `CompensationLedger.value` prices every shift once, over all shifts, and every aggregate on every surface is a plain sum of those per-shift `EarningsComponents`. No consumer recomputes cash, credit, gratuity, tip-out, or wages from stored rows, and no consumer derives one component by subtracting two others.
2. **Every figure names its MetricID.** Every rendered, spoken, exported, notified, or API-emitted number resolves to exactly one registry row or to a `presentation-only` or `analytics:*` tag defined in 1.2. A figure that cannot be named is a gate failure for the PR that introduces it.
3. **Scope and cutoff are explicit.** An `EarningsResult` carries `metric`, `scope` (shift, day, month, pay period, year to date, range), `asOf`, `manifest` digest, `engineVersion`, and `completeness`. `asOf` applies to tips and wages together. Two consumers showing the same metric over the same scope with the same `asOf` and the same manifest digest show the same integer cents.
4. **Wage-inclusive by default, labelled honestly.** Anything labelled "earnings" or "Total" is `earnedIncome`. Figures that exclude wages are labelled "Tips" or "Tips & gratuity". A `.partial` result is never labelled "Total"; a `.unavailable` store state is never rendered as $0.
5. **Observations stay observations.** Stub values are stored and emitted raw. Inferred corrections are separate proposals that require acceptance. Reconciliation deltas are computed per component and never fold into income.
6. **Policy, not device, owns time.** Work dates are civil days in the frozen payroll time zone of the calendar policy in effect; the payroll workweek comes from `PayrollCalendarPolicy`, never from the calendar-grid `firstWeekday`; device time zone changes move nothing.
7. **Every finding has a fixture.** Each divergence in Section 3 maps to a JSON fixture with independently specified expected values. Tests that cannot pass yet list their fixture ID in `Fixtures/KnownIssues.json` and wrap their assertions with `expectingKnownIssues(for:)`; `KnownIssuesGateTests.knownIssueCountIsZero` is required by the release workflow (`PAYDAYCORE_RELEASE_GATE=1`) and fails on any remaining ID. A listed ID whose test already passes fails the ordinary run too (`knownIssueNotRecorded`), because the per-fixture case in `FixtureConsistencyTests.everyFixtureConverts` wraps every fixture in the bundle and `KnownIssuesGateTests.everyKnownIssueIsClaimedByAWrappedTest` rejects a listed ID that names no fixture. So the list only ever shrinks (see docs/CI.md).
8. **The server does no money math.** After PR 6 the backend serves app-published snapshots stamped with a server-issued `dataset_revision`; `/v1/summary` reports `stale` when the revision has moved. Until then, every backend figure in Section 2.14 is nonWageEarnings or a component and is labelled as such.
